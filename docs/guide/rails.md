# Rails Semantic AST Index

DevAgent-TS includes a specialized semantic graph engine for Ruby on Rails codebases.

---

## 12 Scanners

- **Controllers**: Actions, params, filters, and renders.
- **Models**: Associations, validations, scopes, and callbacks.
- **Routes**: URL helpers, verb bindings, and path matching.
- **Jobs & Mailers**: ActiveJob and ActionMailer classes.
- **Policies & Concerns**: Pundit policies and reusable active support concerns.
- **Schema & Migrations**: Database tables, columns, indexes, and foreign keys.
- **RSpec & RuboCop**: Spec files and lint rules.
